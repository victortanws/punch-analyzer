1. What a plot is

A Million Plots is a virtual cemetery of one million numbered plots arranged on a 1,000 × 1,000 grid. Each plot is a single point on that grid. Winning a plot grants you the right to display one memorial there — a name, optional dates, an optional epitaph, and a colour — for as long as this site exists. It is a commemorative display, not real property, real estate, burial rights, or any interest in physical land.

2. How plots are won — the auction

Unclaimed plots are allocated by ascending auction. The first qualifying bid opens an auction with a countdown; further bids must exceed the current high bid by the stated minimum. A bid placed near the end extends the countdown briefly to discourage last-second sniping. When the countdown ends, the highest bidder wins the right to claim the plot by paying their winning bid within the stated claim window and submitting a memorial. Bidding is a genuine commitment to buy at your bid price if you win.

3. If a winner does not pay

If a winner does not complete payment within the claim window, they forfeit the plot and it is offered to the next-highest bidder; if no bidder remains, the plot returns to unclaimed and may be auctioned again. A claimed and paid plot is permanent and is never re-auctioned.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never receive, see, or store your full card number. You are charged only when you claim a plot you have won, for the amount of your winning bid.

5. Refunds

A plot is delivered the moment your payment clears, so completed purchases are final. Two exceptions: (a) a genuine technical failure in which you paid but no plot was assigned — we will assign it or refund you; and (b) the rare case where the same plot was assigned to another buyer before your payment settled — we will refund you in full. We aim to resolve either within 5 business days. Placing or losing a bid never results in a charge, so there is nothing to refund for bidding.

6. What a memorial may not contain

  • Slurs, hate speech, or content that harasses, threatens, or defames.
  • Impersonation, or another living person’s private information.
  • Advertising, links, or spam.

We may remove or redact a memorial that violates these rules without refund, at our discretion. If a memorial names or depicts you or someone you represent and should not, contact us and we will address it.
