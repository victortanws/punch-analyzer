Terms, Privacy & Refunds

Last updated:

Operated by Tan Weng Seng, Selangor, Malaysia. Written plainly, and meant.

1. What Rainbow Bridge is

Rainbow Bridge is a place to remember a companion animal you’ve lost. When you place a pet here, a memorial — their name, optional dates, a few words, and an optional photo — is added to the public meadow. It is a commemorative display, not a product, service, or any interest in property.

2. Review before it appears

Every memorial is read by a person before it is shown, so the meadow stays gentle and free of anything hurtful. Your payment is taken at checkout and your memorial waits in a short queue until approved — usually within a day. If we decline a submission (see section 6), we refund it in full.

3. Permanence — our promise

Once a memorial is approved it stays for as long as this site exists. We will never remove a memorial to sell the space again, and a memorial never expires, dims, or is withheld for non-payment — there is no renewal you must keep paying. We intend this site to last a very long time; we cannot promise the internet is forever, and we won’t pretend otherwise, but we will not quietly delete what you left here, and if we ever had to close we would give notice and a way to keep what you placed.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never receive, see, or store your card number. A place in the meadow is $19; an eternal light is $29.

5. Refunds

Grief is not a moment for fine print. If you placed a memorial and want to undo it, made a mistake, or simply changed your mind, write to us and we will refund you — no explanation needed. We would far rather refund a memorial than have anyone feel taken advantage of. A memorial we decline in review is always refunded. This is more generous than the law requires, and we mean it.

6. Keeping the meadow gentle

Please place memorials only for real companions, in earnest. We may decline or remove anything hateful, harassing, impersonating, advertising, or otherwise inappropriate; where we do, a refund is available. You should share only a photo that is yours to share. We may remove or edit a memorial on request from the person who placed it, or to address a genuine complaint.

7. Your words and photo

By placing a memorial you grant us a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to store and display the name, words, and photo you submit on this site, and you confirm the photo is yours to share. You keep all other rights. If you give us your email for an anniversary remembrance, we use it only for that and never share or sell it; you can ask us to remove it any time.

8. Privacy

We store only what you choose to leave — the memorial details, an optional photo, and an optional email for the yearly remembrance — plus the transaction reference Stripe returns. Card details are handled entirely by Stripe under their privacy policy. We keep basic server logs for security, do not sell your data, and will remove a memorial and its data whenever you ask.

9. No warranty; limitation of liability

The service is provided “as is.” To the fullest extent permitted by law, our total liability for any claim relating to it is limited to the amount you paid us in the preceding 30 days.

10. Changes & contact

We may update these terms; the “last updated” date reflects the current version. For anything at all — questions, corrections, refunds, or removal: victortanws@gmail.com. We read every message.
