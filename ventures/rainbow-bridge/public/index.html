Our promise

Rainbow Bridge is a place to keep a companion close after they’re gone. When you place a pet here, they stay — we will never remove a memorial to sell the space again, and a memorial does not expire or dim if you never return. Every memorial is read by a person before it appears, so the meadow stays gentle. If you ever want a memorial changed or taken down, write to us and we’ll take care of it. Payments are handled by Stripe — we never see your card number.

And if the loss is heavy right now, please know you’re not alone — losing a companion is real grief. Pet-loss support lines and communities exist to sit with you through it; a gentle place to start is the ASPCA’s pet-loss resources.
