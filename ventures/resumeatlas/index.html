ATS Tips

  • 1

    Keep it single-column.Tables, text boxes, and multi-column layouts confuse ATS parsers and can scramble your content order.

  • 2

    Mirror the job posting's language.Use the exact skill and title keywords from the listing — ATS matching is often literal string matching.

  • 3

    Use standard section headings."Work Experience," "Education," "Skills" parse reliably. Creative headings like "My Journey" often don't.

  • 4

    Save as PDF from a print-safe layout.Avoid PDFs exported from design tools with embedded images of text — parsers need selectable text.

  • 5

    Quantify achievements."Reduced onboarding time by 35%" beats "Responsible for onboarding" for both ATS scoring and human reviewers.

  • 6

    Spell out acronyms once.Write "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)" so you match keyword searches for either form.

  • 7

    Avoid headers/footers for key info.Some parsers skip header/footer text entirely — keep contact details in the main body.
