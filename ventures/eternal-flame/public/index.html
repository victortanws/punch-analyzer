What lighting a flame means

Nine dollars lights one flame, permanently. It stays on this wall for as long as the site exists, and every flame ever lit remains reachable — older flames are never deleted to make room. If something here needs our attention, or if you regret a dedication, write to the keepers and we will make it right; we would rather refund a flame than argue beside one. Payments are handled by Stripe — we never see your card number, and we store only the words you choose to leave.
