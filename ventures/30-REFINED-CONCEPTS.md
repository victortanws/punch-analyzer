# The Final 30 — Chair's Ruling

**Selection logic.** I aggregated all four expert scorecards (Monetization, Operator, User, Psychology) into a 0–40 composite for each of the 62 ideas, then culled anything carrying an *unfixable* fatal flaw — gambling/money-transmission structures, anonymous free-text or unscanned-image moderation swamps, unkeepable multi-decade delivery promises, and pure-novelty hooks with no renewing drive. Nine ideas were **rescued-by-fix** (a take-rate carved from a charity split, a tangible physical deliverable swapped in for an intangible promise, a private-by-default toggle to kill a shaming hazard, or a hostage/decay mechanic de-fanged), and near-duplicate twins were merged into the stronger sibling. The surviving 30 are deliberately spread across eight themes — no theme exceeds six — so the portfolio is a durable, recurring-revenue business rather than 30 variations on a memorial wall. The spine of the list is the debate's hardest-won lesson: **grief, gifting, and good (charity/warm-glow) open wallets when there's a real artifact and a real recurring occasion; leaderboards, decay-loops, and "own a pixel" abstractions do not.**

Composite scores below are shown per idea; where a fix raised a score, the pre-fix flaw is named in the "Fixed" line.
